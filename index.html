THE JOURNEY THROUGH TIME

Indian History

🏺

Ancient India

Ancient India witnessed the rise of the Indus Valley Civilization, Vedic culture, powerful kingdoms and important philosophical traditions.

🏰

Medieval India

Medieval India saw the rise of several kingdoms and empires, including the Delhi Sultanate, Vijayanagara Empire and Mughal Empire.

🇮🇳

Modern India

Modern Indian history includes colonial rule, the independence movement and India's journey toward becoming a republic.

PEOPLE WHO SHAPED INDIA

Historical Personalities

👑

Chandragupta Maurya

Founder of the Maurya Empire and an important figure in ancient Indian history.

🦁

Ashoka

Mauryan emperor whose reign became associated with the spread of Buddhism and the use of the Ashoka Chakra.

⚔️

Rani Lakshmibai

The queen of Jhansi who became a prominent figure during the uprising of 1857.

🇮🇳

Mahatma Gandhi

A major leader of India's independence movement who advocated nonviolent resistance.

A GLIMPSE THROUGH TIME

Historical Timeline

c. 2500 BCE

Indus Valley Civilization

One of the world's earliest urban civilizations developed in the northwestern regions of the Indian subcontinent.

c. 320 BCE

Maurya Empire

Chandragupta Maurya established one of the largest empires in ancient India.

1526

Mughal Empire

Babur established Mughal rule in northern India after the First Battle of Panipat.

1947

Indian Independence

India became independent from British colonial rule on 15 August 1947.

1950

Republic of India

India adopted its Constitution and became a republic on 26 January 1950.

ABOUT THIS WEBSITE

Preserving India's Heritage

India has a long and diverse history spanning thousands of years. Its heritage includes architecture, languages, literature, art, music, traditions and cultural practices.

This website is designed as an educational resource for students and anyone interested in learning about India's history and heritage.
